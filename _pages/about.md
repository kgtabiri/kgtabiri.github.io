Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Economics at [Carleton Uviersity](https://carleton.ca/economics/people/kwasi-tabiri/). My primary research interest is in health economics. I am interested in issues related to the development of health capital and the economic consequences of shocks to human capital in both developing and developing countries.
